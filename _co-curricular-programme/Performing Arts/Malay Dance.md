<p>Malay Dance CCA at Evergreen Primary School aims to develop students’
interest in the traditional art form of Malay cultural dance.</p>
<p>Students learn the fundamental movements and techniques of various Malay
dance genres, such as Asli, Inang, Joget, Masri and Zapin. Training sessions
begin with basic dance techniques before gradually progressing to more
advanced choreography. Through regular practice, students develop flexibility,
poise, balance, coordination and stage presence.</p>
<p>Beyond dance skills, the CCA also nurtures important values and dispositions
such as resilience, self-discipline, teamwork and appreciation for culture
and the performing arts. Students learn to express themselves confidently
while gaining a deeper understanding of Malay heritage and cultural traditions.</p>
<p>Our dancers are given opportunities to showcase their talents at various
school events, celebrations and community performances. They also participate
regularly in the Singapore Youth Festival, where they gain valuable stage
exposure and experience. Through these platforms, students build self-confidence,
learn to support one another, and grow together as a team.</p>
<p>Through Malay Dance CCA, we hope to provide our students with an enriching
learning experience that nurtures them as confident performers, gracious
team players and appreciative learners of culture and the arts.</p>
