<p>Chinese Dance CCA at Evergreen Primary School provides students with opportunities
to develop their passion for dance while learning more about this beautiful
and expressive art form.</p>
<p>Our training programme focuses on developing strong foundational techniques
in Chinese Dance, while helping students build flexibility, strength, stamina
and stage presence. Through regular practices, students learn to move gracefully
and in harmony as a group. These shared experiences also help to foster
discipline, teamwork, perseverance and strong bonds among members.</p>
<p>Students are given a wide range of performance opportunities to showcase
their talents and build confidence. These include school-based events such
as the Chinese New Year Concert, Achievement Day and Open House, where
our dancers play an important role in representing the school.</p>
<p>Members also participate in the Singapore Youth Festival Arts Presentation,
a key platform that allows them to strive for excellence and gain valuable
experience performing at a national level. In addition, students may have
opportunities to perform at external events, further broadening their exposure
beyond the school.</p>
<p>Through Chinese Dance CCA, our students are nurtured to become confident
performers, disciplined learners and gracious team players who appreciate
the richness of culture and the performing arts.</p>
