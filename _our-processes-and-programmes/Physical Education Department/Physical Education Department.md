</p>
<p></p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Purpose</strong>
</p>
<p>In Evergreen Primary School, the Holistic Health Framework (HHF) guides
us to bring together our key programmes, structures and processes that
develop the cognitive, affective, moral, physical, and social health of
our students.</p>
<p><strong>Process</strong>
</p>
<p>The HHF focuses on holistic development and well-being which goes beyond
weight and fitness to encompass the holistic development and domains of
students’ well-being. We hope to develop the knowledge, skills, values
and attitudes of every student to live healthily through quality delivery
by trained teachers and qualified learning partners.</p>
<p><strong>People</strong>
</p>
<p>Our school PE department collaborates closely with our learning partners
like Health Promotion Board, Singapore Heart Foundation, SportSG/ActiveSG
and others to carry out programmes to develop students holistically through
awareness and activities.</p>
<p>Coaching by Teachers - 1-to-1 mentoring programme for students who need
more support</p>
<p>This programme allows PE teachers to coach, encourage and motivate students
to adopt and sustain their healthy lifestyle habits via a regular platform
for students to meet the teachers to share their progress and lifestyle
habits. This programme focuses on behaviour modification with the purpose
of weight loss in the long run and not immediate.&nbsp;</p>
<p>We strongly encourage parents to play an active role in the journey by
sharing health and fitness related information and talks to help the students
cultivate good lifestyle and eating habits.</p>
